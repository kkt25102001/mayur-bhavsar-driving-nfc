Do I really learn directly on my own car?

Yes! We train you 100% on your own car (Manual or Automatic) right at your doorstep anywhere in Surat. Ordinary driving school cars have dual clutch/brake on the trainer's side, which gives false confidence. On your own car, you build real judgment and habit from Day 1.

What are the training days and batch schedule?

Training runs Monday to Friday every week. Gold Package is 10 Days (2 consecutive weeks, Mon–Fri), and Silver Package is 5 Days (1 week, Mon–Fri). Saturday and Sunday are reserved for practice and rest.

What if I have heavy driving fear or am 35+ years old?

Mayur Sir has 13+ years of specialized experience training beginners and 35+ age clients with phobias. With step-by-step psychological reassurance and patient techniques, even the most nervous students drive confidently in heavy traffic within 10 days!

Which areas in Surat do you cover for doorstep training?

We provide personal doorstep pickup and drop across all major Surat areas including Adajan, Vesu, Pal, Piplod, Citylight, Katargam, Rander, Varachha, Ghod Dod Road, Althan, and nearby localities.
